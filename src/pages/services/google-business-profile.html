How it works

  1. We check whether a profile already exists for your business.
  2. We create it, or request ownership of the existing one.
  3. Google verifies the business. Google chooses the method, often a short video of your premises or signage, and it can take a few days or longer.
  4. We complete the details and link your website.

Questions

Do I need a shop or office?

No. Businesses that visit customers can hide their address and list the areas they serve instead.

Can you get me more reviews?

We'll show you how to ask happy customers for reviews and how to reply to them. We never write or buy reviews: it breaks Google's rules and can get a profile suspended.
