What we collect

  • Quote requests: your name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the type of business, the services you're interested in, your budget band, when you'd like to start, your preferred contact method and anything you write in the message box.
  • Messages you send us by WhatsApp, email or phone.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider records basic request data, such as IP address, browser type and the page requested, in its server logs. We do not use these logs to identify you.

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising tags or tracking pixels, and stores nothing in your browser. Our fonts are hosted on this site, so loading a page doesn't send your details to a font provider.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your request, prepare a quotation and, if you become a client, deliver and support the work you asked for. Processing your quote request is necessary to take the steps you asked for before a contract (POPIA section 11(1)(b)). We don't send marketing or sell, rent or share your information for anyone else's marketing.

Information sent outside South Africa

Some of these operators store data outside South Africa, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on POPIA section 72, using providers that are bound by data protection laws or agreements giving comparable protection. [[Confirm for each operator]]

How long we keep it

Quote requests that don't become projects are deleted after [[e.g. 12 months]]. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires, for example for tax purposes, and then deleted.

How we protect it

Access to our inbox and accounts is limited to {{founder}} and protected with strong passwords and multi-factor authentication. The website runs over an encrypted (https) connection.
