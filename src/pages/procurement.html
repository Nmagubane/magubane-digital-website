Sending us an RFQ

Email the RFQ or bid documents to {{email}}, or use the quote form and give the reference number and closing date. Please include:

  • The RFQ or tender reference number
  • The closing date and time, and how responses must be submitted
  • The specification or bill of quantities
  • Any compulsory briefing session or site visit
  • The returnable documents required (SBD or MBD forms)

We confirm receipt, and tell you promptly if we will not be responding, so you can plan around it.

How we deliver

Each quotation states who delivers each part of the scope: Magubane Digital itself, or a named partner working under our management. Work is delivered to a written specification, tested with you, and handed over with documentation. How we work

Conflicts of interest

If Magubane Digital writes or advises on a specification, it does not bid on that tender. Directors and their interests are declared on the SBD 4 or MBD 4 form with every bid.
