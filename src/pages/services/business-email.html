SPF, DKIM and DMARC in plain words

SPF lists the servers allowed to send email for your domain. DKIM signs each message so the receiver can check it wasn't changed on the way. DMARC tells receivers what to do with mail that fails those checks, and sends you reports about it.

Since 2024, Gmail and Yahoo have required at least SPF or DKIM from everyone who sends to them, and all three from bulk senders. Setting all three up properly helps your mail get trusted and makes your domain harder to impersonate.
