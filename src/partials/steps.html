1. Survey and requirements

    We visit or meet, record what you have and what you need, and agree the requirement in writing.

  2. Specification and quotation

    A written specification and quotation: scope, items, timeline and price, with who delivers each part. [[Deposit or payment terms]]

  3. Delivery

    We build, install and configure to the specification, with one named contact and regular progress updates.

  4. Testing and acceptance

    Everything is tested against the specification with you, and signed off before handover.

  5. Handover and support

    You get a handover pack with your accounts, network details and warranties, and support under an agreed SLA.
