How we work

The same method on every job, from one classroom to a multi-site roll-out, so you always know what is happening, who is doing it and what you will have at the end.

The five steps

What's in a handover pack

  • Scope delivered, against the specification
  • Test results and the signed acceptance
  • Accounts and admin access, held by you
  • Network diagram and configuration
  • Asset register with serial numbers and warranties
  • Licences and renewal dates
  • User guides and training records
  • Support contacts and the SLA

Running the project

  • One named contact at Magubane Digital for the whole job
  • A written plan with milestones, agreed at the start
  • Regular status updates, with a risk and issue log on larger jobs
  • Changes to scope agreed in writing before they are done
