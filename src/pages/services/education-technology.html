Classroom technology that teachers actually use

Smart classrooms, computer labs, learning platforms and device roll-outs for schools and colleges. Set up, supported, and backed by training for teachers.

Request a quote Procurement information

Scope

Supply, installation, configuration, training and support for:

  • Smart classrooms: displays, teacher devices, connectivity
  • Computer labs and device charging
  • Learning platforms: Microsoft 365 Education, Google Workspace for Education, Moodle
  • Learner and staff accounts
  • Device roll-outs with management and content filtering
  • School websites and parent communication
  • Teacher ICT training
  • Technical support for schools

How it's delivered

Platforms, accounts and device set-up by Magubane Digital. Cabling and wall-mounted equipment installed with partners.

Informal teacher sessions are run by Magubane Digital. Credit-bearing training goes through a MICT SETA-accredited provider. [[Training partner, once agreed]]

Who it's for

Your school has devices or panels that are not being used.

You need learner accounts and a learning platform before next term.

Your district is rolling out smart classrooms and needs a local implementation partner.

What the school gets at handover

  • Accounts list and admin access for the school
  • Device register with serial numbers and warranties
  • Network diagram
  • Training attendance register
  • Support contacts and the SLA
