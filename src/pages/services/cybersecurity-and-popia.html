Security and POPIA, in plain steps

Find out where you are exposed, fix the basics that stop most incidents, and put in place the records and policies POPIA expects.

Scope

Assessment, implementation and ongoing review of:

  • Security assessment, with a written report and priorities
  • Multi-factor sign-in and account hardening
  • Device patching and encryption
  • Backup and disaster recovery, with test restores
  • Email security: SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • POPIA readiness: personal information register and procedures
  • ICT security policies
  • Staff awareness training

How it's delivered

Delivered in-house by Magubane Digital for small and medium organisations.

Who it's for

You hold learner, patient or client records and are not sure they are protected.

You have had a phishing or ransomware scare.

An auditor or your department has asked for an ICT security policy.

Standards we work to

  • CIS Critical Security Controls, Implementation Group 1, as the baseline for smaller organisations
  • The conditions for lawful processing in POPIA
