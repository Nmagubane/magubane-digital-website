Small business packages

For professional practices, SMMEs and start-ups: a website on your own domain, business email, a Google Business Profile and monthly care, in three fixed-scope packages that build on each other.

Every project gets a written quotation. Prices are "from" because every business is different; your quotation fixes the price before work starts. All prices exclude VAT.

What the packages cover

Website

Custom-designed, mobile-first and fast, with a WhatsApp button and an enquiry form that come straight to you.

Business email

Microsoft 365 on your own domain, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC so your mail reaches inboxes and is harder to fake.

Google Business Profile

Set up and verified, so you show on Google Maps and local search with the right hours and contact details.

Domain and DNS

Your .co.za or .com registered or moved, renewed on time, and its records set up correctly.

Monthly care

Hosting, monitoring, renewals and update hours each month, so the site stays online, secure and current.
