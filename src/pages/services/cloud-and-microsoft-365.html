Scope

Supply, configuration, migration and support of:

  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, including education editions
  • Identity and sign-in with Microsoft Entra ID and multi-factor authentication
  • Device management with Microsoft Intune
  • Email on your own domain with SPF, DKIM and DMARC
  • Migrations from on-site servers, Gmail and other providers
  • Backup of cloud email and files
  • Domain registration, renewals and DNS management
  • Licensing advice and supply

How it's delivered

Planned, configured and documented in-house by Magubane Digital. Licences are supplied through [[licensing partner or distributor]] or bought directly by you.

Who it's for

Staff or learners share accounts, or use personal email for work.

Nobody can say which laptops you own, or whether they are secure.
